### How to Get in Touch
Interested candidates are invited to send an email to `jongseok.lee@ntu.edu.sg` with the subject line `[Prospective PhD/Postdoc] Your Name - Position Title`. Please attach the following materials:

* **Projects & Work Samples (Primary Focus):** Links to work you are particularly proud of—such as open-source code repositories (GitHub/GitLab), published or pre-print papers, competition placements, thesis, or personal portfolios. If a project is not publicly hosted, include a brief statement summarizing the work and your explicit individual contribution.
* **Cover Letter:** A brief 1-2 pages statement of research interest explaining your background and why you are a good fit to the position.
* **Curriculum Vitae:** Updated CV detailing your education, key technical projects, and contact information for 2–3 academic or professional references. (Please include publication records, awards, or honors if applicable.)
* **Academic Transcripts:** B.Sc. and if applicable M.Sc. transcripts (scans or official copies). If available, please include your official class rank or percentile (e.g., Top 5%, 3rd out of 120).

Please note that **Incomplete applications will not be considered.** Due to the volume of inquiries, only shortlisted candidates will be contacted for an interview.

## Visiting Students, Internships & External Master Research

I warmly welcome undergraduate and Master’s students — both local and international — who are eager to gain hands-on research experience in robotics and machine learning.

Whether you are seeking a research internship, a visiting position, or an external thesis collaboration, we are always excited to hear from motivated students!

### How to Get in Touch
If you would like to join us, please send a friendly email to `jongseok.lee@ntu.edu.sg` with the subject line `[Prospective Student/Intern] Your Name - Institution`. Please attach:

* **Cover Letter / Brief Note:** Tell us a bit about yourself, what research areas excite you, and your target start date/duration.
* **Curriculum Vitae:** A CV highlighting your background, skills, and projects you are proud of.
* **Academic Transcript:** Recent transcript (scan or unofficial copy).

> *Note for current NTU students: * If you are an NTU student interested in an FYP, URECA, Master's dissertation or robotics project, please reach out directly via email or follow the standard school allocation procedures.
