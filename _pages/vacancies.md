## How to Apply

Interested candidates are invited to send an email to `jongseok.lee@ntu.edu.sg` with the subject line `[Prospective PhD/Postdoc] Your Name - Position Title`. 

Please attach the following materials in a single PDF (or include links):

* **Cover Letter:** A brief statement of research interest explaining your background and why you wish to join RUNES lab (or a 1-page proposal for wildcard applications).
* **Curriculum Vitae:** Updated CV including your publication record, key projects, and awards.
* **Academic Transcripts:** B.Sc. and M.Sc. transcripts (scans or official copies).
* **Code / Work Samples:** Links to GitHub repositories, thesis, or representative publications.
